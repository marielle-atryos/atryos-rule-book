Employee & Contractor Handbook & Workplace Guidelines

A shared reference for how we work together — company-wide standards that apply to everyone, plus specific guidelines for our Dubai, Budapest, Poland, and Manila teams. Select your office location below to view what applies to you.

🌐 Company-Wide Standards

The following standards apply to every Atryos and Astreos employee and contractor, regardless of office location.

🚫

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Discrimination, harassment, or any form of inappropriate behaviour will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action.

🔒

Confidentiality

All employees and contractors are bound by the confidentiality terms outlined in their signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).

🤝

Professional Conduct & Communication

Employees and contractors are expected to contribute to a professional, respectful, and constructive working environment with management, colleagues, suppliers, and customers alike, and to communicate in a professional and constructive manner at all times. When engaging with colleagues — particularly those new to the Company — please exercise sound judgment and allow others the space to form their own views and experiences.

🩺

Health & Safety

All employees and contractors are expected to follow all safety guidelines to help maintain a secure and healthy working environment for everyone.

⏱️

Time Tracking Tool

The Company's time tracking tool must be used consistently, at all times.

🕒

Use of Working Hours

Employees and contractors are expected to use working hours responsibly and remain focused on their duties, responsibilities, and Company priorities. Personal activities should be limited to reasonable breaks and should not interfere with productivity, availability, deadlines, meetings, or the responsibilities of colleagues.

💻

Use of Company Communication Tools

Company communication tools should be used professionally and appropriately, in a manner that supports effective teamwork and productivity. Reasonable social interaction is welcome; however, excessive personal conversations, gossip, inappropriate content, or communication that distracts from work should be avoided. Repeated or disruptive misuse may result in disciplinary action, particularly if it continues after a prior warning.

📝

Pre-Approved Overtime Only

Overtime that has not been pre-approved will not be considered valid.

🗓️

Leave Requests

A single day of leave may be requested in advance or the day before. Requests for more than two days must be submitted at least two weeks in advance. While the Company's HR dashboard is still in development, all requests should follow the format below — no additional explanation is required.

  • Shipping: Mathieu & Dori (Marielle in CC)

📌 Please use this exact format until the HR dashboard becomes available.

🎯

Performance & Goals

Employees and contractors are encouraged to strive toward their set goals. Managers will provide ongoing support and regular feedback to help each team member grow and improve.

📚

Learning & Development

Employees and contractors are encouraged to take advantage of available training opportunities to build their skills and stay current with industry developments.

📱

Responsible Social Media Use

Please be mindful of your online activity and avoid posting content that could negatively impact the Company's reputation.

🤒

Sick LeaveUpdated

📆

Maximum Consecutive Holiday

Up to 21 consecutive week days.

🌴

Annual Leave EntitlementUpdated

24 days per year, plus 1 additional day for every completed year of service, up to a maximum of 4 additional days — as outlined in the employment contract.

🚫

Untaken Holidays

Unused holiday days do not carry over to the following year and cannot be exchanged for cash.

💸

🔄

Off-Setting Public Holidays

⏳ Overtime & Extra Hours

🌙

Staying Late to Finish WorkUpdated

Staying late to finish a task should be a maximum of 1 hour. In that case, you may arrive or leave an hour early later that same week — except on Fridays. If more than 1 hour is needed, it must be approved in advance by Mathieu and Marielle, who will confirm when and how the extra time can be claimed. We encourage avoiding extra hours unless truly critical (e.g. testing a same-day dev release). Please inform Marielle and Mathieu.

⛔

No Accumulating Extra Time

Extra hours cannot be accumulated over a month to take a full day off — annual leave should be used for that purpose instead.

⚖️

Claiming Negative Hours

If an employee or contractor ends their working day early due to illness, an emergency, or another valid reason, the missing hours must be fully recovered within the same week and cannot be spread across multiple days. Otherwise, the affected day will be deducted from their annual leave balance.

🌪️ Emergency Provisions

🌪️

Natural Calamities & Unplanned Circumstances

If an employee or contractor is unable to attend the workplace due to a natural calamity, severe weather, or other unforeseen event, the following will apply, in order:

  • Use of the employee's or contractor's two (2) Remote Work Credits
  • Use of additional eligible days based on tenure
  • Use of available annual leave
  • Unpaid leave, once all other options are exhausted

Budapest

⏰ Attendance & Time Tracking

Attendance & Punctuality

Remote Work Policy

🏖️ Leave & Holidays

🤒

Sick LeaveUpdated

A medical certificate is required at all times to validate sick leave. Compensation follows the terms specified in the employee's or contractor's contract.Note: statutory sick-pay support in Hungary is also governed by Hungarian labour law — the Company pays the first 15 working days per calendar year, after which the state sickness benefit (táppénz), administered through NEAK, may apply.

📆

Maximum Consecutive Holiday🇭🇺 Per Hungarian Labor Law

Up to 21 consecutive week days.

🌴

Annual Leave EntitlementUpdated

Calculated per the Hungarian leave formula (based on age and number of children), plus 1 additional day for every completed year of service, up to a maximum of 5 additional days — as outlined in the employment contract.

🚫

Untaken Holidays🇭🇺 Per Hungarian Labor Law

Annual leave should generally be taken in the year in which it is earned. Unused holiday does not normally carry over to the following year, except where permitted or required by Hungarian law. Holiday entitlement cannot be exchanged for cash during employment; however, any eligible unused accrued holiday will be paid out upon termination of employment, as required by law.

💸

Unpaid Leave🇭🇺 Per Hungarian Labor Law

Employees and contractors may request up to 5 working days of discretionary unpaid leave per calendar year, subject to Company approval. This limit does not apply to statutory unpaid leave or other leave entitlements provided under applicable Hungarian law.

🔄

Off-Setting Public Holidays🇭🇺 Per Hungarian Labor Law

Requests to work on a public holiday and take an alternative day off may be considered where permitted under applicable local law. Requests must be submitted in advance and approved by Dori and Mathieu, with Marielle in CC.

⏳ Overtime & Extra Hours

⚖️

Claiming Negative Hours

Where an employee or contractor is unable to complete their scheduled working hours due to a personal emergency or another approved reason, any resulting negative hours should normally be recovered within the agreed working-time arrangement, subject to prior approval and applicable working-time and rest-period requirements. Absence due to illness or any other circumstance covered by a statutory leave entitlement will be treated in accordance with applicable Hungarian law and will not be considered negative working hours.

🌪️ Emergency Provisions

🌪️

Natural Calamities & Unplanned Circumstances

If an employee or contractor is unable to attend work due to a natural calamity, severe weather, or another unforeseen or unavoidable circumstance, the absence will first be assessed in accordance with applicable Hungarian law. Where the absence is not otherwise covered by a statutory entitlement or exemption, the following options may be applied, as appropriate:

  • Use of any eligible additional company-provided days
  • Use of available annual leave
  • Unpaid leave, where applicable

Employees and contractors should notify the Company as soon as reasonably possible if such circumstances prevent them from attending work.
